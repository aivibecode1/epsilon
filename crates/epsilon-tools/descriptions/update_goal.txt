Change the session's goal. Call get_goal first: goal_id and revision must be the exact pair it
returned, and a stale pair is refused rather than applied.

Actions:

- edit — rewrite the objective and/or the round cap. The phase does not change. Use this when the
  objective was right but the wording or the budget needs to move; use it to raise
  max_goal_rounds when the cap is reached.
- pause — stop continuing. The state is kept, and the human can resume it.
- resume — start continuing again, and clear a former blocker. Use it only when the human asked
  to continue.
- complete — the objective is achieved. Say so in your reply as well: this records it, it does
  not explain it.
- blocked — a concrete condition has to change before work can continue: a missing credential, a
  decision only the human can make, a failing service outside the workspace. Give a short
  lower-kebab-case code and say what the condition is. Blocking is refused before three rounds of
  work: being stuck on the first obstacle is not a blocker, and difficulty, uncertainty or
  remaining work are never reasons to block.
- clear — remove the goal. Its history stays in the session file.

Creating and editing a goal rewrite what the session is for. Do that when the human asked for it,
and ask before replacing an objective they set.
