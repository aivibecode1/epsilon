## Adapted (deliberately different, reason recorded)

- **YOLO is presets, not `/yolo`.** `danger-full-access` + typed-`yes` gate (ADR-032) is the
  safe-configurable-YOLO the goal asks for; a bare `/yolo` word would be a second name for the
  same posture. No developer problem is left unsolved by the absence of the spelling.
- **`/editor` refused by name** in the window (36/37 + 1 refusal) — no native editor bridge exists.
- **Write-through saves, no dirty flags** (P-260) — nothing to mark unsaved.
- **Trash branch dead on Windows**, unlink behind confirmation instead (P-237) — honest about
  the platform rather than pretending a recoverable copy.
- **12-row caps, tail for text / head for diffs** (P-142/P-156) — the skill's cap with Pi's
  bash-card rule, not Pi's numbers.
- **Live transcript filters 3 of 5** (P-294); the `/tree` picker does all five.
